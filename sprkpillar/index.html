The model

How the money is actually made.

We're partnered with a set of apps that pay for one thing: conversions. Every time an ad you run sends them someone who takes action, you get paid for that person.

01

The apps pay per action

A conversion can be a form filled out, an app downloaded, or an offer completed. Different apps count different actions.

02

Up to $20 a conversion

Depending on the app and the action, a single conversion can pay up to twenty dollars. Volume is what turns that into a real number.

03

Ads are how you get them

You run short-form ads on TikTok, Facebook and Instagram that funnel people into taking that action. That's the entire job.

Put simply

Money goes into ads. Ads send people to the app. The app pays you per person who converts. Your job is making that math work in your favour — and everything we're about to show you exists to help you do that faster.

The model

What you're actually doing day to day.

This is where most people expect something harder than it is. You're not building a brand and you're not on camera.

You start from a proven angle

An angle is the approach an ad takes — the hook, the caption, the style, the graphics. We hand you the angles that are already working right now for other people running these same offers. You're not guessing what converts. You're rebuilding something that already does.

You never show your face
  • No face on camera, ever
  • No voiceovers, no talking-head videos
  • Footage is sourced from external places, not filmed by you
  • You assemble the creative — you don't star in it
The whole loop

Take a working angle → build the creative from sourced clips → launch it on the dashboard → keep what performs, kill what doesn't, scale the winners.

The Mentorship08 / 11
The funding program

Prove it works, and we fund the scale.

The usual ceiling on something like this is your own bank account. A campaign is printing, and you can't feed it. That's the problem this solves.

Step 01 · You prove it

Hit a 2× return

Get a campaign returning at least double what you put into it. You can prove that on a small budget — it's the number that matters, not the size.

Step 02 · We fund it

Our money, not yours

We pay the ad spend out of our own pocket to scale it — and we keep feeding it, well past ten thousand, for as long as it keeps returning.

Step 03 · We split it

50/50 from there

Profit from the funded spend is split evenly between you and us. Our money is on the line next to yours, so we only win if it keeps working.

Why this exists

Put $50 into an ad and double it, and the only thing standing between you and doing that at a hundred times the size is capital. We'd rather supply it and take half than watch a winner stall.
