Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information SPRK Network collects, why we collect it, and how you can manage your data. By using SPRK, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly (account details, contact information, submissions, support messages) and information collected automatically (device data, IP address, approximate location, browser type, usage logs, and interaction data). If you connect third-party accounts (e.g., TikTok), we may receive profile and performance data as permitted by those platforms.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate, maintain, and improve SPRK; personalize prompts and recommendations; process payouts; communicate updates; detect fraud and abuse; comply with legal obligations; and support creators and partners.

Legal Bases (where applicable)

Depending on your region, we rely on contract performance (to provide the service), legitimate interests (to secure and improve SPRK), consent (where required for analytics/cookies), and legal obligations (for tax, accounting, and compliance).

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share information with service providers and vendors (hosting, analytics, payments, communications, fraud prevention) under confidentiality and data protection terms. We may disclose information to comply with law, enforce our terms, protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.

SMS / Mobile Messaging

Mobile/SMS opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS opt-in data and consent may be shared only with service providers used to deliver messages on behalf of SPRK Network.

When you opt in to receive SMS communications from SPRK Network, we collect your mobile phone number and your consent record. This information is used solely to send you the messages you agreed to receive (see our SMS Terms in the Terms of Service). We do not use your SMS opt-in data for any other purpose, and we do not sell or transfer it to any third party for advertising, marketing, or promotional use.

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for session management, security, preferences, analytics, and performance. You can control cookies through your browser. Some features may not work correctly without essential cookies.

Analytics & Third Parties

We may use privacy-focused analytics to understand usage. Third-party embeds or platforms you connect to are governed by their own policies; review their terms for details.

Data Retention

We retain information as needed to operate SPRK, meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When data is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it where feasible.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No online service is perfectly secure; please use strong passwords and protect your accounts.

Your Rights & Choices

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data, and in some regions, object to or restrict certain processing, or request portability. You may unsubscribe from non-essential communications. To exercise rights, contact us.

International Transfers

Data may be stored or processed in locations outside your country. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for cross-border transfers.

Children

SPRK is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child provided data, contact us to remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the revised version with a new date and, where required, provide additional notice.
